1. CASE METADATA & PROCEDURAL POSTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Procedural Origin:
    On the evening of 02.11.2025 at 19:10 hours, information regarding a physical 
    brawl in Gali No. 5, Sultanpuri was entered in the General Diary of PS Mangolpuri 
    vide DD No. 42A. First Information Report No. 1190/2025 was subsequently registered 
    on 03.11.2025 at 10:15 hours under Section 115(2) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 
    2023 (BNS), pursuant to the written complaint of injured Dharmveer and Medico-Legal 
    Case (MLC) Certificate No. 4471 from Sanjay Gandhi Memorial (SGM) Hospital, Mangolpuri.

1.2 Arrest & Detention Safeguards (BNSS Section 35(3)):
    The charged offence under Section 115(2) BNS provides for a maximum sentence of 
    simple or rigorous imprisonment for a term up to one (1) year, or fine up to 
    ten thousand rupees (Rs 10,000), or both. Because the offence carries punishment 
    strictly below seven years, IO HC Rajbir Singh complied with mandatory statutory 
    safeguards by serving a Notice of Appearance under Section 35(3) of the Bharatiya 
    Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS) upon the accused, Pappu Chauhan, on 03.11.2025. 
    The accused joined the investigation on 04.11.2025; no custodial arrest was effected.

1.3 Summary Trial Jurisdiction & Compounding Status:
    The case is placed before the Court of Judicial Magistrate First Class / Metropolitan 
    Magistrate, Rohini Courts, Outer District, Delhi. Under Section 283 of BNSS, 2023, 
    any offence not punishable with death, imprisonment for life, or imprisonment 
    exceeding three years qualifies for summary trial disposal. Section 115(2) BNS is 
    further compoundable under Section 290 BNSS (First Schedule) by the person to whom 
    hurt is caused, either unilaterally or through a court-referred Lok Adalat settlement 
    under Section 20 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (LASA).

2. COMPLAINANT'S VERSION & MEDICAL RECORD (MLC NO. 4471)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Factual Narration by Dharmveer (Complainant):
    - Incident Occurrence: Dharmveer states that on 02.11.2025 at around 6:30 PM, he was 
      returning home from the Sultanpuri wholesale vegetable market carrying two cloth 
      grocery bags. Upon entering Gali No. 5 (an 8-foot-wide residential alley), he found 
      a black Hero Splendor motorcycle (Registration No. DL-8S-NB-4219) parked centrally, 
      obstructing pedestrian movement.
    - Altercation: Dharmveer knocked on the door of H.No. 47 and requested that the owner 
      clear the thoroughfare. Accused Pappu Chauhan emerged and allegedly hurled abusive 
      epithets, stating that the street did not belong to the complainant's father.
    - Allegation of Assault: Dharmveer claims that when he protested against the profanities, 
      Pappu Chauhan picked up a heavy, curved steel bucket handle (approx. 14 inches in length) 
      lying near a masonry washing platform and swung it deliberately. Dharmveer alleges he 
      received two violent blows on his left shoulder, followed by an overhand strike that 
      struck the left side of his forehead above the eyebrow, causing profuse bleeding.
    - Dharmveer fled the lane with his neighbour Ramesh assisting him, proceeding directly 
      to Sanjay Gandhi Memorial Hospital, Mangolpuri.

2.2 Clinical Examination & Medico-Legal Findings (MLC No. 4471):
    - Facility & Timing: Sanjay Gandhi Memorial Hospital, Mangolpuri, Casualty Dept. 
      Patient brought at 19:45 hours on 02.11.2025 by friend Ramesh.
    - Examining Medical Officer: Dr. A. K. Meena, Casualty Medical Officer (CMO).
    - Objective Injury Findings:
      "1.5 cm x 0.3 cm lacerated wound with irregular, ragged margins located 1 cm above 
       the left eyebrow. Fresh oozing of blood present. Surrounding local erythema of 0.5 cm. 
       No skull fracture, no depressed bone, no loss of consciousness, no vomiting, pupils 
       bilaterally equal and reactive to light. Simple blunt trauma injury."
    - Evidentiary & Medical Discrepancy:
      The medical examiner explicitly performed an anatomical physical checkup. 
      CRITICAL DISCREPANCY: The clinical chart records ZERO external visible marks, ZERO 
      contusions, ZERO abrasions, ZERO ecchymosis (bruising), and NO swelling or local 
      tenderness on the left shoulder or upper arm. Despite the complainant's specific 
      allegation of enduring two forceful blows delivered with an iron/steel bucket handle 
      to his left shoulder, no clinical manifestation was detected 75 minutes post-incident.

3. ACCUSED'S DEFENCE VERSION (TENDERED 04.11.2025)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Written Defence Statement of Pappu Chauhan:
    - Ownership of Vehicle: Pappu Chauhan states that the Hero Splendor motorcycle belonged 
      to his younger brother, Deepak Chauhan, who had arrived from Nangloi five minutes 
      earlier to deliver five sacks of dry rations for an upcoming family ceremony. The bike 
      was parked against the outer wall of H.No. 47 with sufficient room for walking.
    - Complainant's Aggressive Conduct: Pappu states that Dharmveer arrived in an agitated, 
      intoxicated, or aggressive state, began shouting abuses, and before anyone could 
      displace the motorcycle, Dharmveer forcefully kicked and pushed the motorcycle over. 
      The motorcycle toppled onto the concrete pavement, causing its left rear-view mirror 
      to shatter and fracturing the left front turn-indicator light housing.
    - Accidental Origin of Laceration: Pappu states that hearing the crash of the bike, he 
      rushed out and seized Dharmveer by his shirt collar to prevent him from vandalizing 
      further property. A mutual grapple and push-and-pull ensued on the narrow cobblestone 
      passage. In the course of this physical tussle, Dharmveer lost his balance on the wet 
      cobblestones, stumbled backward, and struck the left side of his forehead violently 
      against the exposed, sharp iron angle-iron support frame of the community Delhi Jal 
      Board (DJB) water tanker delivery stand situated outside H.No. 44.
    - Absolute Denial of Weapon: Accused categorically denies possessing, touching, or 
      wielding any bucket handle, rod, or blunt weapon, asserting that the injury was the 
      sole result of an accidental impact during a mutual scuffle initiated by Dharmveer.

4. WITNESS DEPOSITIONS & CREDIBILITY FACTORS (U/S 180 BNSS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Smt. Sunita Devi (Independent Neighbour, aged 46, resident of H.No. 45, Gali No. 5):
    - Spatial Vantage: Was standing directly at the threshold of her ground-floor main door, 
      approximately 10 to 12 feet from the spot, with an unobstructed view.
    - Deposition Highlights (Recorded 03.11.2025):
      - Heard loud shouting regarding bike parking. Saw Dharmveer push the motorcycle over, 
        followed by both men grabbing each other's collars and grappling violently in the lane.
      - Saw NO weapon, NO rod, and NO bucket handle in the hands of either Pappu or Dharmveer. 
        Both men were grappling with bare hands.
      - Explicitly noted hearing a distinctive, hollow, metallic "THUD" sound when Dharmveer 
        reeled backward during the pushing and clattered into the hollow iron railing / 
        standpipe frame of the Delhi Jal Board water tank.
      - Observed Dharmveer clutch his forehead as blood appeared, whereupon the scuffle ended.
    - Credibility Assessment: Independent eyewitness; no prior animus or kinship with 
      either party; residence immediately adjacent to the locus in quo.

4.2 Shri Ramesh (Complainant's Associate, aged 35, resident of Gali No. 8, Sultanpuri):
    - Deposition Highlights (Recorded on 08.11.2025):
      - Claims he was walking towards Gali No. 5 when he witnessed Pappu Chauhan assault 
        Dharmveer with a metallic strip / bucket handle.
    - Credibility Vulnerabilities:
      - 6-Day Unexplained Delay: Incident occurred on 02.11.2025; Ramesh did not submit a 
        statement to the police until 08.11.2025, despite having accompanied Dharmveer to SGM 
        Hospital on the night of 02.11.2025.
      - Interested Witness Bias: Admits to being a close personal associate and co-worker 
        of Dharmveer at the same textile packaging unit for four years.
      - Obstructed Line-of-Sight & Twilight Darkness: Ramesh was positioned near the corner 
        of Gali No. 5, approximately 20 metres (over 65 feet) distant. A three-wheeled auto-
        rickshaw (TSR) was parked between his position and the locus, partially occluding 
        his field of view. The incident occurred at 6:30 PM in November under dim 20-watt 
        municipal sodium streetlamps. In cross-examination during inquiry, Ramesh could not 
        specify the length, color, or eventual disposal of the alleged bucket handle.

5. INVESTIGATION & EVIDENTIARY GAPS (BSA, 2023 / BNSS, 2023)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Missing Digital Video Evidence & Section 63 BSA Non-Compliance:
    - Existence of 14-Second Video: Aman (aged 17, resident of H.No. 49, 1st floor) captured 
      a continuous 14-second video clip of the scuffle on his Redmi smartphone from his 
      terrace.
    - Investigative Default: On the morning of 03.11.2025, Aman displayed the clip to IO 
      HC Rajbir Singh. However, the IO did not seize the smartphone, did not extract the video 
      to a pen drive, did not compute a cryptographic hash value, and failed to record a 
      certificate under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (admissibility 
      of electronic records).
    - Unavailability of Primary Source: On 05.11.2025, Aman left Delhi for his ancestral 
      village in District Samastipur, Bihar for Chhath festivities, taking the uncopied phone 
      with him. Consequently, direct digital verification of whether a weapon was used or 
      whether Dharmveer struck the water tanker frame remains unavailable to the court.

5.2 Non-Recovery of Corpus Delicti / Alleged Weapon:
    - No Seizure Memo u/s 185 BNSS: The IO conducted a site search of Gali No. 5 and the 
      premises of Pappu Chauhan on 03.11.2025 but failed to discover, recover, or seize any 
      steel bucket handle, iron rod, or blunt weapon. No recovery memo exists in the police docket.

5.3 Forensic & Physical Evidence Omissions:
    - Failure to Swab Delhi Jal Board Stand: Despite the accused immediately raising the 
      defence that the complainant's laceration resulted from striking the angle-iron frame 
      of the DJB water tanker stand, IO HC Rajbir Singh never inspected the water stand for 
      trace evidence, bloodstains, or epithelial tissue, nor were forensic swabs collected.
    - Absence of Mechanical Inspection Report: The motorcycle (Hero Splendor DL-8S-NB-4219) 
      was brought to PS Mangolpuri, but no formal mechanical inspection was conducted by an 
      auto-technical expert to verify whether damage to the left indicator and mirror matched 
      a heavy physical toppling impact, as claimed by the accused.

6. STATUTORY & EVIDENTIARY ISSUES FOR JURYX PANEL EVALUATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 4 advisory jurors must deliberate across Round 1 and Round 2 on the following issues 
without pre-judging the ultimate verdict:

- ISSUE 1 (Mens Rea vs. Mutual Grapple): Did the accused intentionally cause hurt under 
  Section 115(2) BNS, or did the injury occur accidentally during an escalating mutual brawl 
  triggered by the complainant kicking over a parked motorcycle?
- ISSUE 2 (Medical Impeachment of Complainant): Does the complete absence of shoulder 
  trauma on MLC No. 4471 falsify the complainant's narrative of an armed assault with a 
  bucket handle, creating reasonable doubt under Section 4 BSA, 2023?
- ISSUE 3 (Independent vs. Delayed Witness Credibility): Between independent neighbour 
  Sunita Devi (corroborating the metallic thud against the tanker frame) and delayed friend 
  Ramesh (6-day delay, obstructed sightline), which testimony satisfies legal scrutiny?
- ISSUE 4 (Restorative Justice & Compounding u/s 290 BNSS): Given the petty neighbourhood 
  nature of the dispute between co-residents of Gali No. 5, does this matter warrant summary 
  trial conviction, clean acquittal on the benefit of doubt, or referral to Lok Adalat 
  under Section 20 LASA with mutual restitution for the broken motorcycle and medical expenses?
