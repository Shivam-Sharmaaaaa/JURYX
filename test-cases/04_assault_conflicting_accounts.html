1. Case Metadata & Procedural Posture

1.1 Summary Trial Eligibility u/s 283 BNSS: The present proceeding originates from an FIR registered under Section 115(2) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (corresponding to erstwhile Section 323 IPC). Since the maximum statutory punishment prescribed is imprisonment not exceeding one year or a fine up to ₹10,000, the matter satisfies the mandate of Section 283 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS), rendering it summarily triable without formal charge framing.

1.2 Safeguards u/s 35(3) BNSS in Lieu of Arrest: In accordance with Section 35(3) BNSS (codifying arrest guidelines for offences punishable under 7 years), Head Constable Rajbir Singh served a formal Notice of Appearance on 03.11.2025. Accused Pappu Chauhan duly joined inquiry proceedings on 04.11.2025; no physical custody was sought or executed.

1.3 Compounding & Lok Adalat Feasibility: Under Section 290 BNSS (First Schedule), an offence under Section 115(2) BNS is compoundable at the instance of the injured person (Dharmveer) without requiring leave of court. The dispute is eligible for amicable referral to Lok Adalat under Section 20 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (LASA).

2. Complainant's Version & Medical Record (MLC No. 4471)

2.1 Alleyway Parking Confrontation (02.11.2025, ~18:30 Hrs): Complainant Dharmveer deposes that on 02.11.2025 at dusk (around 6:30 PM), while returning from the weekly Sultanpuri mandi carrying household rations, he found an 8-foot-wide public thoroughfare in Gali No. 5 blocked by a black Hero Splendor motorcycle (DL-8S-NB-4219). Complainant knocked on H.No. 47 asking the owner to park responsibly.

2.2 Allegation of Armed Assault: Dharmveer alleges that accused Pappu Chauhan emerged aggressively, used abusive language, and seized a detached steel bucket handle (approx. 14-inch heavy curved wire/rod) from near a municipal washing slab. Dharmveer alleges Pappu Chauhan delivered two heavy strikes on his left shoulder, followed by an overhead blow that struck his left forehead above the eyebrow, causing profuse bleeding.

MLC No. 4471 Findings (Sanjay Gandhi Memorial Hospital, Mangolpuri):
- Arrival Time: 02.11.2025 at 19:45 Hrs (approx. 75 minutes post-incident).
- Clinical Note: "1.5 cm x 0.3 cm lacerated wound with irregular margins above left eyebrow, fresh bleeding, simple blunt injury. Patient conscious, oriented, vitals normal."
- CRITICAL MEDICAL DISCREPANCY: The medical officer conducted a complete physical check and recorded zero visible marks, zero contusions, zero abrasions, and no tenderness on the left shoulder, completely lacking clinical corroboration for the alleged two forceful bucket-handle blows to the shoulder.
3. Accused's Defence Version (Tendered 04.11.2025)

3.1 Legitimate Unloading of Goods: Accused Pappu Chauhan submitted a formal written statement explaining that the Hero Splendor motorcycle belonged to his younger brother Deepak, who had arrived 10 minutes prior to deliver grain bags for a family puja. The vehicle was leaned against their wall, leaving room for pedestrians.

3.2 Complainant's Aggression & Property Damage: Pappu contends that Dharmveer arrived in a belligerent temper, aggressively kicked the motorcycle over onto the stone pavement, shattering the left rear-view mirror and smashing the left front indicator assembly.

3.3 Accidental Impact with Delhi Jal Board Tanker Stand: Pappu states he rushed out to stop further destruction of the vehicle, leading to mutual grappling and push-and-pull. In the struggle, Dharmveer lost his footing on the damp cobblestones and struck his left brow against the sharp iron angle-iron support frame of the Delhi Jal Board (DJB) community water tanker stand located outside H.No. 44. Pappu vehemently denies possessing, using, or discarding any bucket handle or weapon.

4. Witness Depositions & Credibility Factors (u/s 180 BNSS)
  • Smt. Sunita Devi (Independent Neighbour, H.No. 45, Gali No. 5):
    Position: Ground-floor threshold, 10–12 feet from incident; clear, unobstructed view.
    Statement: Corroborates hearing a dispute over bike parking. Saw both men grappling with bare hands; firmly denies seeing any bucket handle, rod, or weapon. Distinctly heard a loud metallic "thud" consistent with a person striking the hollow iron water tanker frame.
    Credibility: Neutral, independent, immediate neighbor with zero personal or financial interest.
  • Shri Ramesh (Complainant's Associate, Gali No. 8, Sultanpuri):
    Position: Lane turn, ~20 metres distant; partially occluded by a parked commercial auto-rickshaw (TSR) in dim dusk lighting.
    Statement: Alleges he saw Pappu strike Dharmveer with a metal strip.
    Credibility Vulnerabilities: 6-day unexplained delay in recording statement (08.11.2025); admitted long-time workplace associate of complainant; unable during inquiry to identify weapon color, dimensions, or where it went.
5. Investigation & Evidentiary Gaps (BSA / BNSS)
5.1 Unseized Mobile Video Clip (Section 63 BSA Non-Compliance): Aman (17-year-old neighbor) filmed a 14-second continuous video clip of the scuffle from his first-floor terrace and showed it to IO HC Rajbir Singh on 03.11.2025. The IO failed to seize the phone, failed to extract the digital media, and failed to procure an electronic hash certificate under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023. Aman subsequently traveled to Bihar for Chhath Puja with the phone, causing complete loss of direct video evidence.

5.2 Non-Recovery of Corpus Delicti / Weapon: No steel bucket handle was ever recovered or seized from the alley or Pappu's residence; no seizure memo u/s 185 BNSS exists in the record.

5.3 Lack of Forensic & Mechanical Verification: The IO failed to swab the Delhi Jal Board water tanker frame for bloodstains or tissue to test the accused's accidental collision defence. Furthermore, no mechanical inspection memo was prepared for the motorcycle at PS Mangolpuri to confirm whether the damage resulted from being violently kicked over.

Even Evidentiary Balance — Key Conflict Angles for Advisory Jurors:

  • The Analyst (Evidence & Chain of Custody): Focuses on the objective MLC record (1.5 cm forehead laceration present, shoulder injury totally absent) and the fatal Section 63 BSA investigative lapse regarding the unseized 14-second mobile video.
