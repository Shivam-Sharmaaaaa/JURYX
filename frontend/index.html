    A first pass for routine cases

    JuryX is a decision-support prototype for Indian summary courts. Four jurors examine a case through different perspectives, debate over two rounds and produce an advisory recommendation. A human magistrate always makes the final decision.

    How it works

    Case loaded

    Choose a preset case or enter your own FIR and evidence.

    Round 1 · Initial votes

    All four jurors independently examine the case and record their initial positions.

    Round 2 · Rebuttals

    Jurors review opposing arguments and record their final positions.

    Head Agent review

    The Head Agent checks the statements, identifies gaps and prepares an advisory.

    Magistrate decides

    The magistrate approves, modifies or rejects the advisory and can download an audit report.

    Four lenses and one reviewer

    The Analyst

    Facts, evidence, timelines and consistency.

    The Empath

    Intent, circumstances and humanitarian context.

    The Jurist

    Applicable Indian law, statutory ingredients and procedure.

    The Skeptic

    Weak evidence, missing proof and contradictions.

    The Head Agent

    Reviews all four jurors, flags issues and prepares the advisory. It does not decide the case.

    Built for human oversight

    Advisory only

    The magistrate keeps full authority over every outcome.

    Two-round debate

    Initial positions and rebuttals are recorded separately.

    Audit report

    Case details, votes, debate, review flags and magistrate decision are exportable.
