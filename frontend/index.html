THE AI BENCH

Different roles.
More complete scrutiny.

Each agent has a defined review lens. Their outputs are perspectives for human consideration, not legal findings.

A
01 / FACTS & EVIDENCE

Analyst

Checks timelines, factual consistency, documentary support and evidence links.

E
02 / CONTEXT

Empath

Considers circumstances, possible intent, proportionality and mitigating context.

J
03 / LEGAL FRAMEWORK

Jurist

Examines stated legal provisions and the ingredients that may need proof.

S
04 / COUNTERANALYSIS

Skeptic

Looks for missing evidence, alternative explanations and procedural concerns.
